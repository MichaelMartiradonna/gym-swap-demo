# Gym Swap demo — 90-second phone recording script

Record on the phone in Brave with the screen recorder. Speak slowly; every button name below is the exact on-screen label. Before recording, open the demo URL once in a fresh tab so the sample workout is already seeded, then start the recording on the home page.

**0:00–0:10 — Open the demo**
"This is Gym Swap, a tool I built for swapping exercises when the equipment you planned on isn't available. This is the public demo, so AI explanations are off, but everything else is the real app." Show the banner, then scroll to the saved-workout strip.

**0:10–0:22 — Show the sample workout**
Tap **Resume Sample workout — exercise 1 of 6**. "A workout is already loaded: six lines, pasted as plain text. The app matched each line to the reviewed library and kept my sets and reps exactly as written." Tap **Next** once and back **Previous** so the viewer sees the lines move.

**0:22–0:35 — Open one occurrence and its swaps**
On Goblet squat, tap **See alternatives**. "For a goblet squat it has thirteen reviewed swaps, each with a similarity label. Equipment readiness is kept separate from similarity." Tap the **Two-dumbbell front squat** card.

**0:35–0:50 — Read a Keeps / Changes / Check card**
Read the three lines aloud, roughly: "Keeps: a standing squat with the load in front of the body. Changes: two weights at the shoulders replace one goblet hold. Check: getting the weights to your shoulders may limit the setup." Then: "Every card is written and reviewed by a coach, with its source listed. The swap decision itself is never generated by a model."

**0:50–1:05 — Enter an equipment note**
Tap **Back to workout entry**, then tap into the **Equipment note** box and type (or dictate): "I have two dumbbells and a bench; the Smith machine is busy". Tap **Apply equipment note**. "It reads the note locally and turns it into separate facts: dumbbells, two of them; a bench; Smith machine present but busy. I can edit any of these, and the note only lasts for this visit."

**1:05–1:20 — Use a swap**
Tap **See alternatives**, open **Two-dumbbell front squat** again, and tap **Use this swap**. "Now this one occurrence of the goblet squat is swapped. My original line and its sets and reps are still there, untouched, and the rest of the workout is unchanged."

**1:20–1:30 — Undo it and close**
Tap **Undo swap**. "Undo puts it back. Choices and position are saved in the browser, so I can pick this up again later. Deterministic rules decide; the optional AI, when it's on, only explains from reviewed notes." Stop the recording on the workout screen.

Tips: keep the phone in portrait, turn on Do Not Disturb, and if a tap misfires just keep talking and retry — the flow is forgiving. Total spoken text is about 190 words, which fits 90 seconds at a relaxed pace.
